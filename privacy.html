Migration data

When you use the platform for a migration, Cutover processes the source code, configuration and sample data you grant access to. We do this only to produce the analysis, plans and infrastructure code you ask for.

  • Sensitive samples, such as message logs and data extracts, are redacted before analysis.
  • We do not use your code or migration data to train models.
  • Where the data includes protected health information, our processing is governed by a Business Associate Agreement.
  • You remain the owner of your data. We process it under your instructions.

How we use information
