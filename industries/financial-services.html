Where financial migrations get stuck

Batch jobs nobody owns

Overnight settlement and reconciliation jobs are often undocumented. Cutover traces their schedules, inputs and outputs from the code and the scheduler.

Cardholder data in scope

PCI DSS scope grows when systems move. Cutover maps which components touch cardholder data before the design is locked.

Reporting must not break

Regulatory and internal reports depend on fields that move between systems. Data lineage shows which reports change, and which do not.

What Cutover produces

  • Inventory of applications, batch jobs and integration points
  • Data flow map showing where sensitive fields are stored and moved
  • Change plan with segregation of duties built into the approval steps
  • Terraform for the chosen target, with network isolation and key management
  • Signed report for SOX and internal audit, with a plan hash and rollback steps
