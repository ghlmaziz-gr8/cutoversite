Where manufacturing migrations get stuck

Custom ERP extensions

Years of custom code sit on top of standard ERP. Cutover inventories the extensions and marks the ones to keep, change or retire.

Plant and IT boundaries

Connections between IT and plant systems need clear ownership. Cutover maps each one so the boundary is agreed before work starts.

Supplier integrations

EDI and portal feeds with suppliers break quietly. Cutover traces every feed and its message formats.

What Cutover produces

  • Inventory of ERP, quality, MES-adjacent and supply chain applications
  • IT and plant boundary map with each interface classified
  • Phased cutover plan around production and shipping schedules
  • Terraform for the target, with network segmentation
  • Signed report with rollback steps for each phase
