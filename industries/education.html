Where education migrations get stuck

Calendar-bound cutovers

Registration, grading and financial aid have fixed deadlines. Cutover schedules each phase into the windows that exist.

Many small systems

Campuses often run dozens of departmental tools. Cutover inventories them and separates the ones to move from the ones to retire.

Student data everywhere

Records appear in exports, test databases and logs. Redaction runs before any sample is analyzed.

What Cutover produces

  • Inventory of student, learning and departmental applications
  • Integration map for SSO, grading and finance feeds
  • Term-aware cutover schedule with freeze windows
  • Terraform for the target, with access controls by role
  • Signed report for the registrar, CIO and privacy office
